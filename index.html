KaChing 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

KaChing 是一款 macOS 菜单栏应用。我们不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据，也不做任何追踪或广告。

数据只保存在你的 Mac 上

你填写的月薪、工时、悬赏任务、心愿单、里程碑，以及离席和会议记录，只保存在本机的 App 沙盒容器里，不会发送到任何服务器。删除 App 即可删除全部数据。

自动感知只在本机判断

  • 空闲时长：只读取“距离上次键盘 / 鼠标操作过了多少秒”，不记录任何按键内容。
  • 麦克风是否被占用：只读取默认输入设备“是否正在被其他 App 使用”，不录音、不读取任何声音。
  • 前台应用：只读取当前前台应用的名称和标识，用于区分工作和摸鱼，不读取窗口内容。
  • 锁屏 / 休眠：只接收系统的锁屏、休眠通知。

以上信息都不会离开你的设备。所有自动感知功能都可以在设置里关闭。

网络请求

为了换算货币，App 会向 open.er-api.com 发送一个获取公开汇率的请求，请求中不包含任何用户信息。该服务的隐私处理由其提供方负责。

通知

如果你允许，App 会在本机弹出“已赚出某个里程碑”的系统通知。通知由 macOS 在本地发送。

儿童隐私

App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

政策变更与联系方式

如果本政策有变更，会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。有任何问题，请在 GitHub Issues 中联系我们。

KaChing Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

KaChing is a macOS menu bar app. We do not collect, upload, or sell any personal data, and we do not track you or show ads.

Your data stays on your Mac

Your salary, working hours, tasks, wish list, milestones, and away and meeting records are stored only in the app's sandbox container on your Mac and are never sent to any server. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Automatic sensing happens on-device

  • Idle time: only how many seconds have passed since the last keyboard or mouse input. No keystrokes are recorded.
  • Microphone in use: only whether the default input device is being used by another app. Nothing is recorded or listened to.
  • Frontmost app: only the name and identifier of the frontmost app, to tell work apps from distractions. Window contents are never read.
  • Lock and sleep: only the system's lock and sleep notifications.

None of this leaves your device, and each feature can be turned off in Settings.

Network requests

To convert currencies, the app sends one request for public exchange rates to open.er-api.com. The request contains no user information.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows local macOS notifications when you reach a milestone.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated. For questions, please open an issue on GitHub.
